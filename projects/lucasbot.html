LucasBot

{% include project-badges.html portfolio=page.portfolio %}

Some time ago, my friend approached me with a request to help him with a research project he was undertaking for school. After discussing various ideas, we decided to collaborate on developing a straightforward yet functional Discord bot.


This bot, though simple, incorporated a few entertaining and interactive features. When my friend presented this project to his class, it was very well received, and he ended up earning an excellent grade for his efforts!

{% include scroll-down.html target="features" %}

Features

  • Announces when a new member joins.
  • Responds to greetings such as “good morning.”
  • Accepts virtual cookies.
  • A game of 'Rock Paper Scissors'.
  • A game of 'Number under 10'.
  • Warns users when they use profanity.
  • Shows the current member count.
  • /clearchat command.
