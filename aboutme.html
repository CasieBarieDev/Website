About Me

Hi, I’m Cas Barnard, a -year-old software developer and theater technician from the Netherlands. I started coding when I was young, and I still enjoy turning a small idea into something people can play with or use.


I build games for friends and family, tools for my smart home, and the occasional Discord bot or Minecraft plugin. You can see what I’ve made on the Projects page .


If you have a question about me or one of my projects, use the form below or join the Discord Server .

Personal Life

Ever since I was young, I've been drawn to technology and creativity, always looking for ways to bring ideas to life. That passion eventually led me into the world of theater, where I now work as a theater technician. I love being behind the scenes and helping create unforgettable shows, whether it’s setting the right lighting mood, solving technical challenges during a performance, or making sure everything runs smoothly for the audience. Every show is different, which keeps the work exciting and challenging.


But life isn’t just about work and coding. Some of my favorite moments are the simple ones, hanging out with friends, going on spontaneous adventures, or just relaxing together. I also enjoy editing videos, especially when I can turn fun memories into something we can look back on later. There’s something really satisfying about capturing moments and making them even more special.


Whenever I need a break, I take my dog, Billy, for a walk. It’s the perfect way to clear my mind, get some fresh air, and relax for a bit. Swimming is another hobby that helps me unwind. It keeps me active and gives me time to think, helping me stay focused and energized for new projects.


And through it all, I’m lucky to have an amazing girlfriend who supports me and shares many of my interests. Whether it’s work, hobbies, or everyday life, having someone by my side makes everything even better.
